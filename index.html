2-Ingredient Apple Marshmallows

← Back to all recipes
Level 🟡 Medium
By @stefan_agcoaching
Original Instagram reel
Recipe source Reel caption
Protein 💪 2 g per slice
Makes / time 9 big slices

Ingredients

  • 500 g peeled, chopped apples (creator used 4 Granny Smith)
  • 30 g powdered gelatine → 🌱 agar-agar
  • Optional: 20 g honey (creator says you don't really need it) → 🌱 agave or maple syrup

Instructions

  1. Boil the apples with a little water until soft.
  2. Drain the excess liquid.
  3. Mash or blend until completely smooth.
  4. While still warm, mix in the gelatine until dissolved.
  5. Whip with a mixer until thick, pale and fluffy.
  6. Pour into a lined tray and refrigerate until set.
  7. Slice.

Notes

  • Per slice: 35 kcal · 2 g protein · 7 g carbs · 0 g fat (9 big slices).
  • Creator credits @themodernnonna for the idea.

2-Ingredient Cauliflower Fudge

← Back to all recipes
Level 🟢 Easy
By @lilsipper
Original Instagram reel
Recipe source Creator's website: 2-Ingredient Cauliflower Fudge – lilsipper

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cup steamed cauliflower florets (measure after steaming)
  • 1 cup chocolate chips of choice → 🌱 dairy-free chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Steam the cauliflower.
  2. While it's still hot, put it in a blender with the chocolate chips. No need to melt the chocolate first; the heat from the cauliflower melts it as it blends.
  3. Purée until smooth and creamy.
  4. Transfer to a lined dish and refrigerate for a few hours, or until firm.
  5. Slice and enjoy.

Notes

  • Depending on the chocolate you use, it can be low carb, sugar free, paleo and keto.
  • The creator steams the cauliflower rather than using it raw, for easier digestion.

4-Ingredient No-Bake Brownies (2nd reel)

Level 🟢 Easy
By @organicallyaddison
Original Instagram reel
Recipe source Creator's website: 4 Ingredient Date Brownies – Organically Addison · 🎬 checked against the video on 06/11/2026: same recipe
Makes / time 9 brownies · about 1 h 15 min (mostly chilling)

Ingredients

Brownies

  • 1 cup pitted dates, softened
  • ⅖ cup creamy all-natural peanut butter
  • ½ cup cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp pure maple syrup (or honey)

Topping

  • ½ cup creamy all-natural peanut butter
  • ½ cup dark chocolate chips, melted

Instructions

  1. Line an 8×8 inch pan with parchment paper.
  2. Soften the dates in hot water for at least 10 minutes, then drain.
  3. Process the dates, peanut butter, cocoa powder and maple syrup in a food processor or blender until combined.
  4. Press the mixture into an even layer in the pan.
  5. Topping: stir the peanut butter and melted chocolate together until creamy.
  6. Pour the topping over the brownies.
  7. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour to firm up.
  8. Slice and serve.

Notes

  • The reel's caption is empty, but the video matches this recipe: dates, peanut butter, cocoa powder and a little maple syrup in a food processor, spread in a pan, then a 2-ingredient frosting of melted chocolate + peanut butter.   • You can use almond, cashew or sunflower butter instead of peanut butter, and add chocolate chips.
  • Storage: airtight container in the fridge for up to 7 days.
  • Per brownie (site's nutrition card): 270 kcal, 7 g protein.

4-Ingredient No-Bake Date Brownies

← Back to all recipes
Level 🟢 Easy
By @organicallyaddison
Original Instagram reel
Recipe source Creator's website: 4 Ingredient Date Brownies – Organically Addison
Protein 💪 7 g per brownie
Makes / time 9 brownies · 15 min prep, 75 min total (incl. 1 h in the fridge)

Ingredients

Brownies

  • 1 cup pitted dates (softened)
  • ⅖ cup creamy all-natural peanut butter (amount as written on the site)
  • ½ cup cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp pure maple syrup (or honey)

Topping

  • ½ cup creamy all-natural peanut butter
  • ½ cup dark chocolate chips, melted

Instructions

  1. Line an 8×8-inch pan with parchment paper.
  2. Soften the dates in hot water for at least 10 minutes, then drain.
  3. Put the softened dates, peanut butter, cocoa powder and maple syrup in a food processor or blender. Process until combined.
  4. Press the mixture into an even layer in the lined pan.
  5. For the topping, stir the peanut butter and melted chocolate together in a bowl until creamy.
  6. Pour the topping over the brownies.
  7. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour to firm up.
  8. Slice and serve.

Notes

  • Soaking the dates in hot water softens them so they blend more easily.
  • Almond, cashew or sunflower butter can replace the peanut butter.
  • You can add chocolate chips to the brownie mixture.
  • Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 7 days.
  • Per brownie: 270 kcal · 30 g carbs · 7 g protein · 17 g fat · 5 g fiber.

5-Ingredient Chocolate Pumpkin Pie

← Back to all recipes
Level 🟡 Medium
By @thevegan8brandi
Original Instagram reel
Recipe source Reel caption
Makes / time One 9-inch pie · at least 5 h in the fridge (or overnight)

Ingredients

  • One 9-inch pie crust
  • 400 g semi-sweet dairy-free chocolate chips (not bittersweet)
  • 3/4 cup (180 g) unsweetened plant-based milk
  • 1 cup (240 g) plain pumpkin purée, room temperature
  • 1 tsp pumpkin pie spice
  • 1/4 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. If using a store-bought crust, prebake it for 5 minutes at 375°F (or follow the package instructions). The crust must be pre-baked, since the filling isn't baked.
  2. Make sure the pumpkin purée is not cold, or it won't blend well and will make the melted chocolate seize.
  3. Put the chocolate chips and milk in a microwave-safe bowl. Heat in two 30-second intervals, stirring in between. Stir until the chocolate is almost completely melted into the milk. (Or heat the milk over a double boiler and melt the chips in it.)
  4. Add the pumpkin purée, pumpkin pie spice and salt to a blender.
  5. Pour the melted chocolate into the blender and blend until smooth and pourable. It will be quite thick at first, so stir a couple of times and make sure it's all blended.
  6. Pour into the prebaked crust and smooth the top. Make swirls with the back of a spoon.
  7. Refrigerate at least 5 hours or overnight to firm up.

Notes

  • Don't use bittersweet chocolate, or the pie will be too bitter and dense/hard.
  • Creator used a store-bought vegan graham cracker crust; homemade is fine too.
  • Serve as is or with dairy-free whipped cream. Fully vegan.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 oz bags freeze-dried blueberries (as written; she found 1 1/2 bags gives more blueberry flavor). Use freeze-dried, not dried.
  • 3 egg whites (or 1/2 cup carton egg whites; some people have trouble with carton egg whites) → 🌱 6 tbsp aquafaba (2 tbsp per egg white)
  • 1/4 tsp cream of tartar
  • 1/3 cup water
  • 1 1/4 cups maple syrup
  • 3/4 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of sea salt

Instructions

  1. Make sure the stand-mixer bowl is very clean, with no grease, or the egg whites may not whip.
  2. In the stand mixer, whip the egg whites until frothy, about 1 minute. Add the cream of tartar and salt, then whip to soft peaks (a few minutes).
  3. Meanwhile, blend the freeze-dried blueberries in a food processor or blender to a powder with no clumps. Once the egg whites reach stiff peaks, add the blueberry powder and whip 1 more minute. Turn off the mixer while you make the syrup.
  4. In a medium/large pot, combine the water and maple syrup. Bring to a boil over medium heat and keep boiling until it reaches 240°F (115°C). Try not to stir; if it bubbles too high, lower the heat slightly. It must reach 240°F or the fluff will deflate later.
  5. Take the syrup off the heat. With the mixer on medium-high, pour it very slowly into the egg whites. When all the syrup is in, turn the speed up and whip until thick and glossy, 2–3 minutes.
  6. Add the vanilla and whip 1 more minute. Transfer to a large jar or container.

Notes

  • Best within 3–4 days; keeps up to 1 week on the counter. You can re-whip it if it goes flat.
  • Swap: you can replace 1/4 cup of the maple syrup with honey.
  • Needs a stand mixer (she says a hand mixer works too) and a candy thermometer.
  • Paleo, dairy-free, no corn syrup. Not vegan (egg whites).

Char Siu Seitan

Ingredients

Seitan

  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2/3 cup vegetable broth
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 tsp miso paste
  • 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
  • 1/2 tsp asafoetida (hing)
  • 0.25 tsp ground white pepper
  • 2 cups vital wheat gluten

Marinade

  • 2 cups vegetable broth
  • 1.5 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp five spice powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tsp rice vinegar
  • 2 slices fresh ginger

Sticky char siu glaze

  • 3 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1/4 cup vegetarian hoisin sauce
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 3 slices fresh ginger
  • 1/2 tsp asafoetida (hing)
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp sambal oelek
  • 1/2 tsp Chinese five spice powder
  • 1 tsp beet powder (optional, for the classic red colour)
  • 1/2 cup water

Instructions

  1. Blend the cannellini beans (with their liquid), soy sauce, vegetable broth, sesame oil, miso, nutritional yeast, white pepper and hing until completely smooth.
  2. Put the vital wheat gluten in a large bowl. Pour the bean mixture over it and mix by hand until a dough forms. Knead and gently stretch about 1 minute, just until smooth. Don't over-knead, or it gets dense.
  3. Shape the dough into a long rectangle and set aside in a container while you make the marinade.
  4. Combine all marinade ingredients in a large pot and bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Reduce to a gentle simmer.
  5. Carefully add the seitan and simmer 30 minutes. Keep it at a gentle simmer, not a rolling boil, or the seitan gets dense and chewy. It will absorb most of the liquid.
  6. While it simmers, whisk all glaze ingredients together in a medium bowl.
  7. Move the seitan to a cutting board, slice into strips, then cut each strip into 2–3 inch pieces.
  8. Heat a drizzle of sesame oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the seitan in a single layer and cook 2–3 minutes per side until golden and lightly caramelized. Work in batches if needed.
  9. Return all the seitan to the skillet, pour the glaze over and toss to coat.
  10. Cook 3–5 minutes, stirring often, until the glaze is thick and glossy: about 3 minutes for saucier, longer for stickier and more caramelized. Remove the ginger slices before serving.

Notes

  • Per serving (website): 488 kcal · 50 g protein · 67 g carbs · 5.4 g fat · 8 g fiber. Caption says about $2.05 per serving.
  • Storage: fridge up to 5 days in an airtight container; freezer up to 3 months (with the glaze). Reheat in a skillet over medium heat, or air fry at 375°F for 4–5 minutes.
  • Tastes even better the next day, so good for meal prep.
  • Oil-free option: bake or air fry instead of pan-frying (less caramelized).
  • Serve with: jasmine or brown rice, bok choy, Chinese broccoli, rice noodles, bao buns, lettuce wraps, pickled cucumber or carrots, sesame seeds.

Caramel

  2. In a medium saucepan (non-stick if you have one), mix the sugar and water until the sugar is wet. Use a pastry brush to wet the sides of the pan so the caramel doesn't stick.
  3. Heat on medium, stirring every so often with a heat-safe silicone spatula or whisk, until the sugar bubbles and turns darker. It will look wet, then form stiff bubbles, then melt into clumps, and finally melt into a caramel-coloured syrup. Keep stirring.

Vegan Marshmallows

← Back to all recipes
Level 🔴 Hard
By @tyberrymuch
Original Instagram reel
Recipe source Creator's website: The Best Vegan Marshmallow Recipe – Thank You Berry Much
Makes / time 64 small (or 16 jumbo) marshmallows · 35 min active + 4 h to set

Ingredients

Marshmallow base

  • 1 tbsp modified soy protein (Versawhip 600K, 8 g)
  • ½ cup cold water (120 g)
  • 1 tbsp + 1 tsp vegan gelatin (15 g)
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar (25 g)

Sugar syrup

  • 1 ¾ cups granulated sugar (350 g)
  • ½ cup cold water (120 g)
  • 1 cup light corn syrup (240 ml)
  • ¼ tsp fine salt
  • ½ tsp vanilla paste (or 1 tsp vanilla extract)

Coating & prep

  • ½ cup powdered sugar (60 g)
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch or tapioca starch (16 g)
  • Neutral oil

Instructions

  1. Prep the pan: Line an 8×8-inch pan with parchment, leaving an overhang. Brush the parchment well with oil. Mix the powdered sugar and starch, then dust the oiled pan with 1–2 tbsp of the mixture.
  2. Start the foam: Put the Versawhip and ½ cup cold water in a stand mixer bowl. Whip on high with the balloon whisk for about 8 minutes, until stiff peaks form and it has tripled or quadrupled in volume.
  3. Start the syrup: Meanwhile, combine the 1 ¾ cups sugar, the water and the corn syrup in a saucepan. Stir once, then simmer over medium to medium-low heat without stirring until it reaches 245–250 °F. It reaches 220 °F quickly, then takes several more minutes to climb to 245 °F. Watch it so it doesn't boil over.
  4. Add the vegan gelatin: Mix the vegan gelatin with the 2 tbsp sugar to prevent clumping. Once the foam is at stiff peaks, turn the mixer to medium-low and slowly sprinkle it in until well combined.
  5. Whip in the hot syrup: As soon as the syrup reaches 245–250 °F, slowly stream it in with the mixer on medium-low. Turn up to high and whip until very thick, lighter in color and glossy. It's ready when it falls from the whisk in thick ribbons that spiral onto themselves and hold their shape. If the ribbons sink back in, keep whipping. Mix in the salt and vanilla near the end. This takes about 8 minutes in the creator's KitchenAid, but go by appearance, not time.
  6. Transfer to the pan: Quickly pour into the prepared pan, smooth the top with a well-oiled spatula, and dust with more of the powdered sugar–starch mix. Cool uncovered at room temperature for 4 hours before cutting.
  7. Cut: Lift out using the parchment overhang. Cut into squares with an oiled chef's knife or bench scraper, cleaning and re-oiling between cuts. Toss the squares in the remaining powdered sugar mix and shake off the excess.

Notes

  • Read all the steps before starting, and use a digital thermometer.
  • Versawhip 600K is a modified soy protein, not regular soy protein powder or isolate. It whips like egg whites and is much more stable than aquafaba. It's sold online (e.g. Modernist Pantry). Potato protein gave good structure, but its flavor was too noticeable.
  • Vegan gelatin must be carrageenan-based, not agar agar. Agar makes them rubbery or jelly-like.
  • Corn syrup: light corn syrup is not high-fructose corn syrup. Tapioca syrup should also work.
  • Storage: airtight container at room temperature for up to 1 week. To freeze, dust them well and keep in an airtight container or freezer bag for several months. Thaw at room temperature.
  • Makes about 800 g: 16 jumbo (~50 g each) or 64 small (~12 g each). About 50 kcal per small marshmallow.
